# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely fresh-fruit trade news for a reader who follows fruit supply, wholesale prices, imports and seasons, with particular interest in durian, cherry, and apple.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Market-moving.** A ban, tariff, or inspection rule that changes a major trade lane; a large crop shortfall or surplus; a nationwide price spike or crash; a shipping or cold-chain disruption affecting a whole season.
- **7-8: High value.** A concrete volume, price, or season forecast with numbers and a named origin or market; a new variety or growing practice with measured results; a major importer, retailer, or exporter decision; a port or origin opening a new fruit lane.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Routine arrival reports, single-market price commentary, retail promotions with some supply detail, industry conferences, and trade statistics without a clear consequence.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Marketing copy, generic "fruit is healthy" content, listicles, recipes, and stories that mention fruit only in passing.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, unverified rumor, unrelated food or technology content, and anything where "apple" means the company.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer items with a named variety, origin, volume, price, or effective date over commentary. Seasonal timing matters: an arrival or price report is valuable when it is current and specific, and worthless when it is a month old summary. Price claims from retailers or exporters are claims; independent market data is stronger evidence. Certification, cold-chain, and phytosanitary problems rank high because they can stop a trade lane.

Use three to five specific topic tags, preferring variety and origin names such as durian-Malaysia, cherry-Chile, apple-Shandong.
