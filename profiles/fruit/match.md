# Fruit market profile

Use this profile for news that tells a reader what is happening in the fresh-fruit trade: supply, price, season, imports and exports, phytosanitary and customs rules, variety and planting changes, retail and consumption trends, and company moves in the fruit business.

Priority categories are durian, cherry, and apple, but the profile covers all fresh fruit: citrus, grapes, bananas, mango, blueberries, avocados, peaches, pears, kiwifruit, and tropical fruit generally.

Typical items include a shipment or arrival volume report, a wholesale price move with numbers, a season forecast, a new tariff, ban, or inspection requirement on a fruit trade lane, a variety release or orchard practice change, a retailer or importer announcement, and a quality or cold-chain problem that affects supply.

Do not use this profile for processed food, beverages, restaurants, meal delivery, general agriculture policy with no fruit content, iPhone or technology stories using the word "apple", or pure recipe and lifestyle content.
