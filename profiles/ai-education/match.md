# AI education profile

Use this profile for timely developments where artificial intelligence meets teaching and learning: AI tutoring and teaching assistants, education models and edtech products, curriculum and assessment changes driven by AI, AI literacy policy, classroom deployments and measured learning outcomes, and education-sector funding or regulation that is specifically about AI.

Typical items include a launched or shut-down AI teaching product, a school, district, university, or ministry adopting or restricting AI use, a randomized study or measured result on learning outcomes, a new AI-literacy standard or exam policy, and a credible report on how educators actually use these tools.

Do not use this profile for general education news that never mentions AI, campus administration, admissions rankings, sports, or purely promotional edtech marketing. Do not use it for general AI research or model releases that carry no teaching or learning angle.
